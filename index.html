🌶️ Spicy (18+) orders

Spicy work runs on my own local setup, so a few simple rules keep it safe for everyone. Spicy pricing: +$35 on stills (ref sheets count as stills), +$45 on animation (from $80).

  • Spicy add-ons are only for buyers who are 18+ and have their age or "18+" in their X bio.
  • Accounts under 30 days old or with no post history can't order spicy.
  • I check your profile before quoting.
  • Spicy orders are paid upfront through PayPal or X Money only.
  • I never ask for or store ID photos.
  • I can refuse or cancel any spicy order if I'm unsure, with a full refund.

📋 Terms

  • AI-assisted: all work is made with AI tools and finished by hand. Please don't resell it as hand-drawn art.
  • Your art, your rights: I only edit or animate art that you own or have the artist's written okay for.
  • Payment: 100% upfront before any work starts. No payment plans or deposits.
  • Revisions: 2 small revisions are included; bigger changes are quoted separately.
  • Turnaround: about 3–7 days for stills and audio, and 1–2 weeks for animation and apps.
  • Refunds: full refund if I haven't started yet; partial refund once WIPs have been shown; no refund after final delivery.
  • 18+: spicy work is only for adult buyers who meet the Spicy (18+) order rules (age or "18+" in your X bio, an account at least 30 days old with post history), and all characters must be adults.
  • Will not draw: real people, minors or childlike characters, hate content, or anything illegal.
